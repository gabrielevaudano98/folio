# Record your first meeting

You need Windows, headphones, about 3 GB of free disk space and 15 minutes for the first download.

1. Open **Settings** (`Ctrl+,`), find **Optional features** and turn on **Meeting recorder**.
2. Next to **Speech model**, choose **Download** (about 1.1 GB). Next to **Notes model**, choose **Download** too (about
   1.1 GB) if you want a summary and action items.
3. Choose the **Meeting language**: *Detect* for mixed Italian and English, or a fixed language.
4. Put on your headphones and join the call. Tell everyone you are recording.
5. Choose **Start recording** (or the command *Record a meeting…* in the command palette, `Ctrl+Shift+P`). The first time, Folio
   reminds you about consent and headphones. Give the meeting a title.
6. A bar at the bottom shows *Recording* and the time. Talk as usual.
7. Choose **Stop**. The transcript is saved at once in `Meetings/`; the notes follow within a few minutes.
8. Open the note. Every point ends with *(line n)*: check it against that line before you share it.

If something goes wrong, see [how the meeting recorder works](../explanation/meeting-recorder.md).
